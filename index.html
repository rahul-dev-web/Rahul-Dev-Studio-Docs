About Rahul Development Studio

Rahul Development Studio provides software and digital development services for individuals, creators, startups, and businesses. Projects may range from simple websites to full-stack applications and custom software systems.

  • Full-stack web development
  • Full-stack application development
  • Flutter / mobile application development
  • Discord bot development and automation
  • Custom software and integrations
  • Deployment and technical setup
  • Domain and hosting setup
  • Optional maintenance and post-launch support
02

Services & Additional Work

Services are selected and priced according to the requirements of each project. Deployment, infrastructure, domain, hosting, third-party services, and other operational work may be included or charged separately depending on the agreed scope.

Examples of additional or separately scoped work may include custom backend systems, authentication, payment integrations, APIs, databases, admin panels, advanced automation, third-party integrations, cloud infrastructure, or other features not explicitly included in the project agreement.

No service is considered included solely because it is technically possible within the selected technology stack. The Final Project Agreement defines the actual deliverables.

Reference starting price: Simple static websites start from ₹1,000. This is a reference starting price only; actual pricing may change based on scope, complexity, features, integrations, timeline, and discussion.

Minimum project charge: There is no universal fixed minimum. The minimum applicable amount may vary by project.

Other projectsMilestones may be customized after discussion
Important: The 40% advance is the standard starting structure, not an inflexible rule. The final payment schedule will always be specified in the Final Project Agreement.

Payment methods: UPI, bank transfer, and QR-based payment. Final payment must be cleared before production deployment unless a different arrangement is explicitly agreed in writing.

05

Project Scope & Change Requests

Only deliverables explicitly included in the agreed scope are part of the base project price. Requests outside the agreed scope may be treated as additional work.

  • New features or functionality
  • Additional pages, screens, modules, or workflows
  • Major design changes after approval
  • New integrations or APIs
  • Additional backend/database functionality
  • New automation or third-party services

Additional work will be discussed and priced before implementation wherever reasonably possible.

06

Revision Policy

Two revision rounds are included free with all projects. A revision round means a consolidated set of reasonable changes to the agreed work.

After the included two rounds, additional revision rounds may carry a fixed charge agreed before the work is performed. A completely new direction, feature, or scope change may be treated as additional development rather than a revision.

Changes requested after deployment are generally treated as upgrades, enhancements, or maintenance work and may be charged separately.

07

Timeline & Client Delays

The estimated or committed delivery timeline will be discussed and recorded in the Final Project Agreement. Timeline estimates depend on scope, client responsiveness, content availability, technical dependencies, and other project factors.

  • Clients should communicate delays as soon as reasonably possible.
  • Delays in content, approvals, access, credentials, payments, or required information may shift the delivery timeline.
  • Client-caused delays will be considered when calculating cancellation thresholds and project timelines.
  • Urgent/accelerated delivery may be possible subject to availability and additional charges.
08

Domain, Hosting & Infrastructure

  • Domain registration is charged separately unless explicitly included in the quotation.
  • Hosting will be arranged according to the client's preference and the technical needs of the project.
  • Third-party domain, hosting, API, software, subscription, or infrastructure charges are payable separately unless explicitly included.
  • Client-owned infrastructure/accounts are preferred where appropriate to preserve long-term client control.
  • Deployment and infrastructure setup are project-scope items and may carry additional charges.
09

Source Code, Repository & Ownership

Source code can be provided when requested by the client and when included/agreed as part of the project handover.

If the client requires GitHub repository ownership, the repository can be transferred after the agreed handover conditions and payment obligations are completed.

Rahul Development Studio retains ownership of its pre-existing reusable libraries, generic utilities, development tools, templates, frameworks, internal methods, and components that are not uniquely created for the client's project, unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Project-specific deliverables and access will be handed over according to the Final Project Agreement.

10

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Client cancellation: If a client cancels after the project has reached 50% or more of the agreed project timeline, the advance payment becomes non-refundable.

If the client cancels before reaching that 50% timeline threshold, the advance is fully refundable, subject to the project-specific agreement and any explicitly agreed non-refundable third-party costs.

Client-caused delays: Delays caused by missing content, approvals, access, information, or payments may be excluded from the timeline calculation for the cancellation threshold.

Developer cancellation: If Rahul Development Studio cancels a project, the client will receive a 100% refund of payments made for the project. This developer-cancellation refund is not subject to the 50% timeline threshold.

If the client abandons or stops responding to a project, the project may be terminated under the applicable cancellation and refund terms after reasonable communication attempts.

11

Post-Delivery Support & Maintenance

20-day free bug-fix period: Implementation-related bugs in the delivered work are covered for 20 days after delivery/deployment, as applicable.

A bug fix does not include new features, new requirements, redesigns, content changes, third-party service changes, or modifications requested after approval.

Optional maintenance plans may be offered if the client wants ongoing updates, support, monitoring, or minor changes. Maintenance pricing and scope are agreed separately.

12

Communication & Approvals

Primary communication: WhatsApp. Official documents: Email.

A reasonable target response time is within 24 business hours, subject to workload, weekends/holidays, emergencies, and availability.

Client approvals and confirmations given through WhatsApp may be treated as valid project communication and approval records.

For important commercial or scope changes, written confirmation through email or the project agreement is preferred.

Weekend or holiday work may attract additional charges when specifically requested and agreed in advance.
